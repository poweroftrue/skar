## Design Guidelines

- Similar to Nord Fragrances (https://nordfragrances.com/)
- Show the pod itself (the refill/balm container)
- NOT the case - these are standalone pods
- Clean, minimal product photography
- Transparent background (PNG)
- High resolution for web
- Consistent lighting and perspective across all pods

## Reference
See Nord Fragrances website for style inspiration - they show the oval-shaped solid perfume pods separately from the metal cases.
